# E1-1: CDK assertion test for the voice stack (owner: P4)

**From:** E1 · **Needs:** `infra/cdk/test/voice-stack.test.ts` (P4 owns `infra/cdk/test`)

## Why
`voice-stack.ts` just had a CI-only failure: the stack triggered an AWS availability-zone lookup at synth time, which
passes locally from a cached `cdk.context.json` and fails in CI. An assertion test would catch that class of bug and
pin the drain settings.

## Proposed test
Build `VoiceStack` with `env: { account: '111111111111', region: 'us-east-1' }` and a fake `DataStack`/`EventsStack`, then assert:
- the app synthesizes with no `missing` context entries (no lookups);
- the container has `stopTimeout` 120 and `minHealthyPercent` 100 / `maxHealthyPercent` 200 on the service;
- the task role allows `ecs:UpdateTaskProtection`;
- the container secrets come from `1145/dev/runtime`.
