## What
1. **Turn handling.** In `worker.py`, replace `turn_detection=MultilingualModel(), **session_kwargs()` with
   `turn_handling=turn_handling(MultilingualModel())` (from `frontdesk.voice_config`).
   Why: the pinned livekit-agents 1.8.4 deprecates the flat kwargs (warning on every call), and if anyone later adds
   `turn_handling=` beside them, AgentSession **silently ignores** every flat kwarg, including `turn_detection=`, and
   falls back to `inference.TurnDetector()`. `turn_handling()` puts the detector inside, so that can't happen.
   `session_kwargs()` keeps working (same keys plus `min_interruption_words`, `false_interruption_timeout`,
   `resume_false_interruption`) until this lands.
2. **Filler kind.** In `agent.py` `_with_filler`, pass the lookup kind and our last spoken turn:
   `pick_filler(self._recent_fillers, kind=..., last_agent_turn=...)` with `kind="availability"` for
   `check_availability`, `"booking"` for `book_appointment`, `"info"` for `lookup_business_info`.
   Why: today a parking question can only get generic lines; with the kind, fillers fit the question
   ("Let me double-check that.") and never sound like a scheduling lookup. Without the kind it still works (generic pool).
3. **Pronunciation.** Once E4-2 lands, pass
   `tts_text_transforms=tts_text_transforms(tenant.pronunciations)` to AgentSession, and
   `pronunciation_dictionary_locators=pronunciation_locators(dict_id, version_id)` to `elevenlabs.TTS(...)` when the
   tenant has a provisioned dictionary. Until then, nothing to do.
